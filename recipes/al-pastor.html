Al Pastor Pizza

Yield:8 Servings (1 slice each)

This hearty Mexican-inspired pizza has a Mojo Rojo sauce base topped with al pastor pork, grilled pineapple, roasted red peppers and onions, pickled pearl onions and is garnished with fresh cilantro garnish.

Ingredients

  • 4 fl. oz. Water
  • 2 fl. oz. Red wine vinegar
  • 1 1/2 tsp Sugar
  • 4 oz. Pearl onions
  • 1 Pizza crust
  • Mojo Rojo Sauce
  • 4 oz. Pork al pastor, prepared
  • 2 oz. Roasted peppers and onions
  • 3 oz. Pineapple, sliced, grilled
  • 4 oz. Oaxaca Cheese, shredded
  • Cilantro sprigs, fresh

Directions

  1. For the pickled onions; bring water, vinegar and sugar to a simmer. Add pearl onions and remove from heat. Allow to come to room temperature, and store in refrigerator overnight to marinate.
  2. Preheat convection oven to 425°F.
  3. Spread Mojo Rojo sauce on pizza crust. Top with pork, roasted peppers and onions, pineapple and shredded cheese. Bake at 425°F until crust is golden brown on edges, and cheese is melted. Garnish with pickled onions and fresh cilantro.
